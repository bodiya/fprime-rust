The state machine models in this directory are copied from NASA's F Prime
(https://github.com/nasa/fprime, Apache-2.0, at the commit in
`FPRIME_UPSTREAM_COMMIT`) and generated by `build.rs` into `sm_models.rs`:

- `FppTest/state_machine/` — upstream `FppTestProject/FppTest/state_machine`:
  every `internal` model (`state`, `choice`, `initial`, the `harness`
  types), the `internal_instance` components `state/Basic.fpp`,
  `state/BasicGuardTestAbsType.fpp` and `choice/InputPairU16U32.fpp`, and
  `external_instance/SmTest.fpp`, unchanged;
- `Svc/FpySequencerStateMachine.fppi`, `Svc/Seq.fpp` and
  `Svc/CommsPorts.fpp` unchanged; `Svc/FpySequencerSm.fpp` includes the
  first inside a module `Svc.FpySequencer` (upstream: the component of
  that name), and `Svc/ComAggregatorSm.fpp` is the `AggregationMachine`
  part of `Svc/ComAggregator/ComAggregator.fpp`, verbatim.

`Probe/Probe.fpp` is the `Probe` machine of
`crates/fprime-comp/tests/sm_traces.rs`, written in FPP.
